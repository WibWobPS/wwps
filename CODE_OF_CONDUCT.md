# Code of conduct

## The short version

This is a small hobby project about a mobile puzzle game. Be decent to the
people you are talking to.

## What is expected

- Assume the person on the other side is trying to help.
- Keep criticism about the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change without owing a long defence.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information, including account keys, device
  ids or e-mail addresses taken from a server you operate.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through a GitHub security
advisory or a direct message. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, and may block accounts, when this document is
not respected.

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.
